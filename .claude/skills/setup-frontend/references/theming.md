# Theming (light and dark)

Part of the `setup-frontend` skill. Read it before changing a colour, a theme or the theme toggle.

- Raw palette variables (`--cv-background`, `--cv-surface`, `--cv-line`, `--cv-ink`, `--cv-copy`, `--cv-muted`, `--cv-meta`, `--cv-accent`, `--cv-accent-strong`, `--cv-accent-ink`, `--cv-accent-core`, `--cv-accent-deep`, `--cv-haze`, `--cv-name-light`, `--cv-name-glow-strength`, `--cv-name-glow-spread`, `--cv-danger`, `--cv-wash`, `--cv-overlay`, `--cv-dot`, `--cv-field-line`, `--cv-field-surface`, `--cv-field-border`, `--cv-autofill`, `--cv-light-rest`, `--cv-shade`, `--cv-shadow-panel`) live in two blocks in `src/index.css`: `:root, :root[data-theme="light"]` and `:root[data-theme="dark"]`. Retuning or adding a theme edits only those blocks. The three `--cv-name-*` tokens tune the name's hover effect per theme: the sheen's light colour (the plain accent in light, where ink mixed with it stays near-black), and the halo's strength (a unitless percentage) and spread. The dark block sits inside `@media screen`, so print always uses the light tokens whatever the screen theme is; keep it there, or paper prints dark. A `@media print` rule gives the page `--cv-surface` as its background, also in the margins.
- `--cv-light` in the light block is the strength of the background's directed light in both themes, because the dark block leaves it out. `--cv-light-rest` is the resting band's strength, set in both blocks, because the same accent shows far less on the light page. The band stays behind small text, so measure its contrast after changing it.
- The background's light has three depths of the accent, used by the light utilities and `DotEcho` only, never by text, buttons, fields or surfaces: `--cv-accent-deep` is the haze under each light, `--cv-accent` its surface and `--cv-accent-core` the hot middle of the lit dots. In the dark theme the deep colour is a dark green and the core a nearly white mint. In the light theme they swap roles in lightness (a bright green haze, a deepest-emerald core), because on paper light shows as saturation, not brightness. `--cv-haze` is the haze's strength, set in both blocks (16% light, 30% dark). The haze lies where the accent fades out, with at most a faint trace under a light's peak and none under the small text that stays in the light: the date line has almost no contrast to spare, so measure it after changing `--cv-haze`, the deep colour or a haze gradient, and look at the dark theme for a second band or pool of light beside the first.
- `--cv-field-surface` and `--cv-field-border` are `color-mix`es of the accent over `--cv-surface` (8%) and over `--cv-line` (38%), for form fields and the profile preview. `--cv-autofill` follows the field surface. After changing the accent, Surface, Hairline or Meta, measure `--cv-meta` on the field surface again: it must stay at 4.5:1 or more, which is why the tint is capped at 8%.
- `@theme inline` maps them to Tailwind utilities (`bg-cv-background`, `text-cv-ink`, `border-cv-line`, `bg-cv-overlay`, `shadow-cv-panel`). Components use only these semantic utilities.
- Light theme uses a darker emerald accent for contrast; dark theme uses a mint accent. Both lean a little toward cyan. After changing the accent, check white `--cv-accent-ink` on the light accent (5.5:1 now) and the accent on the page. `--cv-accent-strong` is the primary button's hover fill (deeper in light, brighter in dark) and small accent text on the background's light, such as the CV's job title and the form fields' labels. `--cv-accent-ink` is the text colour on accent fills.
- `ThemeToggle` (rendered by `Background`, top-right) stores the choice in `themeAtom` (`src/state/themeAtoms.ts`, Jotai `atomWithStorage`, key `cv-theme`, default from `prefers-color-scheme`) and sets `data-theme` on `<html>`. An inline script in `index.html` applies the theme before first paint to avoid a flash; keep its storage key in sync with `themeStorageKey`.
- The `Theme` const object lives in `src/types.ts`.
- Two kinds of colour are the same in both themes and sit in `@theme` instead of the theme blocks: the language flags' official colours (`--color-cv-flag-*`), which only `LanguageFlag` uses, and the neutral light `--color-cv-sheen`, which `ProfilePortrait` lays over a photo with `mix-blend-overlay` for its glare and foil, so a photo keeps its own colours.
