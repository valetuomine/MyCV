# Theming (light and dark)

Part of the `setup-frontend` skill. Read it before changing a colour, a theme or the theme toggle.

- Colour values live in two places only: `src/index.css` and the `colors` frontmatter of `DESIGN.md`, which `impeccable` reads as its tokens. Change both together, and never write a value into the prose of `DESIGN.md` or this skill; name the token instead.
- Raw palette variables (`--cv-background`, `--cv-surface`, `--cv-line`, `--cv-ink`, `--cv-copy`, `--cv-muted`, `--cv-meta`, `--cv-accent`, `--cv-accent-strong`, `--cv-accent-ink`, `--cv-accent-core`, `--cv-accent-deep`, `--cv-haze`, `--cv-name-light`, `--cv-name-glow-strength`, `--cv-name-glow-spread`, `--cv-danger`, `--cv-wash`, `--cv-overlay`, `--cv-dot`, `--cv-field-line`, `--cv-field-surface`, `--cv-field-border`, `--cv-pane-lit`, `--cv-pane-far`, `--cv-pane-rest`, `--cv-pane-neutral`, `--cv-rim`, `--cv-autofill`, `--cv-light-rest`, `--cv-shade`, `--cv-shadow-panel`) live in two blocks in `src/index.css`: `:root, :root[data-theme="light"]` and `:root[data-theme="dark"]`. Retuning or adding a theme edits only those blocks. The three `--cv-name-*` tokens tune the name's hover effect per theme: the sheen's light colour (the plain accent in light, where ink mixed with it stays near-black), and the halo's strength (a unitless percentage) and spread. The dark block sits inside `@media screen`, so print always uses the light tokens whatever the screen theme is; keep it there, or paper prints dark. A `@media print` rule gives the page `--cv-surface` as its background, also in the margins.
- `--cv-light` in the light block is the strength of the background's directed light in both themes, because the dark block leaves it out. `--cv-light-rest` is the resting band's strength, set in both blocks, because the same accent shows far less on the light page. The band stays behind small text, so measure its contrast after changing it.
- The background's light has three depths of the accent, used by the light utilities and `DotEcho` only, never by text, buttons, fields or surfaces. The one exception is `--cv-accent-core`, which is also the light theme's edge glow colour (`--cv-edge-glow`). The three depths: `--cv-accent-deep` is the haze under each light (the two have no Tailwind utilities, so read them as `var(--cv-accent-core)` and `var(--cv-accent-deep)`), `--cv-accent` its surface and `--cv-accent-core` the hot middle of the lit dots. In the dark theme the deep colour is a dark green and the core a nearly white mint. In the light theme they swap roles in lightness (a bright green haze, a deepest-emerald core), because on paper light shows as saturation, not brightness. `--cv-haze` is the haze's strength, set in both blocks (16% light, 30% dark). The haze lies where the accent fades out, with at most a faint trace under a light's peak and none under the small text that stays in the light: the date line has almost no contrast to spare, so measure it after changing `--cv-haze`, the deep colour or a haze gradient, and look at the dark theme for a second band or pool of light beside the first.
- `--cv-field-surface` and `--cv-field-border` are `color-mix`es of the accent over `--cv-surface` (8%) and over `--cv-line` (38%). The border frames form fields and cards; the opaque fill is left for the switch track and `--cv-autofill`, which follows it because the browser's autofill colour sits underneath.
- The Pane (`@utility bg-cv-pane`) is the see-through, blurred surface of cards, form fields, floating panels, the language track and the featured card's skeleton. `--cv-pane-lit` is its top-left corner and `--cv-pane-far` the pane it fades to by 40% of the diagonal, both an accent tint over a see-through Surface. In the dark theme the corner is the lighter, more see-through one and the far pane the denser one; in the light theme the corner is the denser, whiter one and the far pane the more see-through, tinted one. `--cv-pane-rest` is the strength of the weaker accent light in the bottom-right corner, a percentage. `--cv-pane-neutral` is the see-through Surface without accent for an invalid field (`bg-cv-pane-neutral bg-none`). Floating panels add `bg-cv-pane-dense`, a 75% Surface under the Pane. A fading ancestor (`opacity` below 1, also during a Framer Motion fade) switches the blur off until it ends, so fade the Pane itself or the content inside it, never a wrapper around it. They are see-through gradients, not single colours, so they have no hex values in the `DESIGN.md` frontmatter. After changing the accent, Surface, Muted, Copy or a Pane token, measure `--cv-muted` (the fields' placeholder) and `--cv-copy` where the Pane is weakest for dark text: its brightest point in the dark theme and its darkest point in the light theme (the far pane and the bottom-right corner), over the background's light. Both must stay at 4.5:1 or more, which caps the dark theme's lit corner and the light theme's far tint.
- The edge glow and the rim live in `@theme inline`: `shadow-cv-edge` at rest, `shadow-cv-edge-hover` on a mouse hover and `shadow-cv-edge-focus` on `:focus-visible`, all reading `var(--edge, var(--cv-edge-glow))` at `--cv-edge-rest`, `--cv-edge-hover` and `--cv-edge-focus`, so `[--edge:var(--cv-danger)]` turns them danger-coloured. The theme blocks set the colour to the accent tone that stands out from the background (`--cv-accent-core` at 32% / 48% / 60% in the light theme, `--cv-accent` at 24% / 42% / 50% in the dark), so a control never vanishes into the background's light; `shadow-cv-card` is Panel plus `shadow-cv-edge` and `shadow-cv-card-hover` Panel plus `shadow-cv-edge-hover`; `shadow-cv-accent-hover` is the primary button's accent glow plus `shadow-cv-edge-hover`; `inset-shadow-cv-rim` draws a 1px top highlight in `--cv-rim` (white at 80% light, 6% dark). Components add `print:shadow-none` beside them, and put the hover and focus glows behind `not-print:` (`not-print:hover:shadow-cv-edge-hover`, `not-print:focus-visible:shadow-cv-edge-focus`), because a state variant beats `print:shadow-none`; with the same `not-print:` prefix the focus glow comes later in the CSS and wins over the hover glow. Forced colours drop the shadows through the browser; `forced-colors:shadow-none` only states it.
- `@theme inline` maps them to Tailwind utilities (`bg-cv-background`, `text-cv-ink`, `border-cv-line`, `bg-cv-overlay`, `shadow-cv-panel`). Components use only these semantic utilities.
- Light theme uses a darker emerald accent for contrast; dark theme uses a mint accent. Both lean a little toward cyan. After changing the accent, check white `--cv-accent-ink` on the light accent (5.5:1 now) and the accent on the page. `--cv-accent-strong` is the primary button's hover fill (deeper in light, brighter in dark) and small accent text on the background's light, such as the CV's job title and the form fields' labels. `--cv-accent-ink` is the text colour on accent fills.
- `ThemeToggle` (rendered by `PageControls`, which `Background` renders top-right) stores the choice in `themeAtom` (`src/state/themeAtoms.ts`, Jotai `atomWithStorage`, key `cv-theme`, default from `prefers-color-scheme`) and sets `data-theme` on `<html>`. An inline script in `index.html` applies the theme before first paint to avoid a flash; keep its storage key in sync with `themeStorageKey`.
- The `Theme` const object lives in `src/types.ts`.
- Two kinds of colour are the same in both themes and sit in `@theme` instead of the theme blocks: the language flags' official colours (`--color-cv-flag-*`), which only `LanguageFlag` uses, and the neutral light `--color-cv-sheen`, which `ProfilePortrait` lays over a photo with `mix-blend-overlay` for its glare and foil, so a photo keeps its own colours.
