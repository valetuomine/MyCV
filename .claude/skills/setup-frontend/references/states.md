# States

Part of the `setup-frontend` skill. Read it before building a loading, error, empty, not-found, sign-in or sign-out screen.

- Loading, error, empty, not-found, and auth screens render through `RouteMessage`; never show bare unstyled text. `tone` takes the `Tone` const from `src/types.ts`: `Tone.Neutral`, `Tone.Pending` (the dot pulses with `animate-cv-pulse`, hidden under reduced motion; sets `aria-busy`), or `Tone.Error` (danger dot, `role="alert"`). Pass `onRetry`/`showHomeButton` for the default Try again and Open a CV buttons, or `actions` to replace them. Put identifiers such as a public URL segment in `<code>` inside the heading; `RouteMessage` styles it as a chip.
- Every message screen needs a way forward: a failure offers Try again plus a way back (Back to CV or Open a CV); never leave the user on a dead end.
- All sign-in states render through `src/auth/SignInMessage.tsx`: not configured, the method choice ("Sign in with Microsoft" and "Sign in with Google" buttons in each provider's brand colours, a one-line hint, and a "Last used" badge on the last successful method), popup (on devices with a mouse or trackpad the choice opens the provider in a 500 × 640 popup right in the click: "Continue in the Microsoft window" with a focused Cancel, shown without `delayed`), cancelled popup or redirect (the choice again, at once, with a "Sign-in was cancelled." status line and focus back on the method that was tried), cancelled at the provider (neutral tone), failed (error tone, naming the provider, with Use another way when both methods are set up), pending redirect (names what is being signed in to, for example "Signing in to manage `segment`", and the provider the user is sent to), and slow. Touch devices (`(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)`) and blocked popups use the redirect. The pending redirect holds for 3.5 seconds with a focused Cancel that stops it before it leaves (then `aria-disabled`) and Back, in one row of 44 px buttons, so nothing moves when Cancel comes or goes. Coming back from the provider with Back (restored from the back/forward cache, or reloaded on a `back_forward` navigation) shows the choice as cancelled and never starts anything on its own. After 5 seconds without a redirect, the pending screen offers Reload page rather than calling MSAL again, because a second login call while one is in flight fails with `interaction_in_progress`.
- Sign-in popups (`src/auth/signInPopup.ts`) never run the app: `main.tsx` reads the `mycv-popup.` state on the callback routes before the app loads and hands the response to the opening tab (`relayPopupCallback.ts`), which alone stores the session. Microsoft's sign-in page enforces `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy`, which cuts the popup off from the tab, so the app can't detect that window closing or always close it; Cancel therefore also records the attempt in local storage, and a cancelled popup closes itself without signing in. Adding a sign-in method (`linkSignIn.ts`, `SignInMethodsSection`) uses the same popup with a linking state (`mycv-popup.link.`), keeps the session and "Last used", and shows its waiting message with Cancel and its outcome in place; touch devices and blocked popups add it with a redirect. Google's silent renewal stays a redirect. Every sign-in request asks for the app's language (`signInLanguage.ts`: Google `hl`, Microsoft `ui_locales` and `mkt`), and the blank page MSAL opens first takes the app's background, so the Microsoft popup doesn't flash white in the dark theme.
- `Background` renders `SignOutButton`, `LanguageToggle` (FI and EN segments with inline-SVG flags, never emoji flags, which Windows shows as letters) and `ThemeToggle` top-right, in one row that fits beside Sign out on a phone. When signed in with either method, `SignOutButton` shows the account name (email in the `title`) beside Sign out, hidden below `sm`. While a sign-out is under way (`useSigningOutReason()` is set), `Background` replaces the page with `src/auth/SignOutMessage.tsx`, a pending "Signing out" screen matching the sign-in one, which says when the user is signed out for being idle, and `SignOutButton` hides. It shows without `delayed`, because it answers a click, and stays up for the sign-out hold until the reload. `Background` also renders `SignedOutNotice`, a bottom-centred pill that confirms a sign-out for 4 seconds inside an always-present `role="status"` region, and `IdleSignOutWarning`, which for the last minute before an idle sign-out shows a bottom-centred `IdleWarningPanel` (`max-w-md`, `rounded-3xl`, translucent surface with `shadow-cv-panel`): a title, one line of text, a mono `m:ss` countdown, an accent bar that empties linearly with it (no glide under reduced motion), and a primary Continue session that takes focus. The clock and bar are `aria-hidden`; an sr-only live region, also in `aria-describedby`, gives the time left at 60, 30 and 10 seconds.
- Placeholder content uses skeleton shapes, not spinners: every shape is `cv-skeleton` (`src/index.css`), the overlay fill with the shared shimmer, so one light crosses every shape together and stands still under reduced motion. Cards and frames behind them are see-through `bg-cv-wash`, never an opaque `bg-cv-surface`, so the background's light stays visible while loading. On the CV that light is the resting band alone: `ProfileSkeleton` keeps the wash and the beam dark with `useLightHold()` until the name's reveal lights them (`references/layout.md`, The Light's Ignition).
- Every skeleton holds its shapes back for 300 ms and fades them in (`animate-cv-fade-in-delayed` on the visual part), so a load under 300 ms never flashes it. Give that visual part the style from `useSkeletonTiming()` (`src/pages/Profile/components/`), which times the hold and the shimmer from the load's first skeleton, so a skeleton that replaces another during the same load (a route's `Suspense` fallback, then the route's own loading state) doesn't vanish and fade in again. Put a skeleton that shows inside a larger component in its own component (like `SignInMethodsSkeleton`), because the hook must mount and unmount with the skeleton. Keep the sr-only `role="status"` text and `aria-busy` outside that delay, so screen readers hear the load at once.
- A skeleton repeats the layout it stands for: the same page wrapper, grid and spacing, one row per line of text, carrying the text's size and leading with `h-lh` and the bar inside, and the real component's sizes, so nothing moves when the content arrives. Measure the y coordinates of the skeleton and the loaded view at desktop and phone widths; they may differ by about 16 px at most. `ProfileSkeleton` stands for the CV and `ProfilePdfRouteSkeleton` for the PDF view; each route's `Suspense` fallback and loading states use its own.
- The PDF view's loading, failure and viewer states live in `ProfilePdfFrame`, because an `<object>` stays empty while the API prints and its `load` event doesn't tell when the viewer is done. `useProfilePdfLoad` fetches the PDF first and is cancelled by leaving or by a language change, since the frame's `key` is the URL. `ProfilePdfSkeleton` then stays under the transparent `<object>` until the viewer paints over it, with its shimmer off, which covers the round trip of the viewer's own request. The `role="status"` region sits outside the `aria-busy` frame and starts empty, then gets its text 300 ms into a load, when `animate-cv-fade-in-delayed` shows the indicator, so a cached PDF neither flashes nor announces its start. A failure is a `role="alert"` with Try again, which moves focus to the frame. Browsers with `navigator.pdfViewerEnabled === false` skip the fetch and the announcements and get the `<object>`'s fallback at once, which must stay opaque to hide the indicator beneath it.
- Form errors render inline under the field with `role="alert"` and `aria-describedby`, using `text-cv-danger`.
- The profile photo is `ProfilePortrait`: it shows the profile's `pictureUrl` (built into a full URL with `getProfilePictureUrl` from `profileQueries.ts`) when one exists, otherwise a monogram, and falls back to the monogram when the photo fails to load. While the photo loads, the card already has the photo's height, is see-through Wash and carries the skeleton shimmer; its border lights sit in a layer masked to the 2px ring (`mask-cv-ring`), so they don't show through. Then the photo fades and sharpens in. The edit view's `ProfilePictureSection` changes the picture with its own requests, saved at once and apart from Save and Cancel, and words their failures by status code with `getProfilePictureError` (`profileFormUtils.ts`). `preparePicture` (`utils/preparePicture.ts`) shrinks a chosen photo on a canvas before upload. The print route's `data-print-ready` also waits for the photo to load or fail.
